Waterpik UK Homepage Redesign
Website Overview
Waterpik UK is an e-commerce website specialising in water flossers, replacement tips and oral healthcare accessories. The website serves UK customers looking for effective interdental cleaning products, product guidance and customer support.

The redesigned homepage, “The Science of a Healthier Smile,” introduces a product-led, evidence-focused experience. It combines product discovery, specialist product education and clinical credibility to help visitors understand Waterpik products and make informed purchasing decisions.
Main Improvements
1. Improved Product Discovery and Selection
- Introduced a dedicated product finder to help visitors identify suitable water flossers.
- Added clear guidance comparing cordless and countertop water flossers.
- Improved product navigation and purchasing pathways.
Problem addressed: The previous website offered limited guidance to help customers choose between different water flosser types.
2. Clearer Specialist Tip Information
- Created a dedicated visual section featuring six Waterpik replacement and specialist tips.
- Improved the presentation of individual tip types and their intended uses.
- Made specialist product information easier to explore and compare.
Problem addressed: Specialist tip information was difficult to navigate and compare, making product selection less straightforward.
3. Stronger Clinical Credibility
- Brought Oral Health Foundation recognition into a more prominent position.
- Highlighted PrecisionPulse technology and clinical research.
- Connected product education with supporting oral healthcare information.
Problem addressed: Clinical credibility and supporting product information were distributed across different website pages rather than presented as a cohesive trust-building experience.
Design Improvements
- Visual Identity: Applied the approved navy blue primary colour (#0C1C4D), supported by blue, teal and pale blue accents.
- Typography: Retained Sora for headings and Source Sans 3 for body text and interface elements.
- Homepage Layout: Introduced a more structured, product-focused layout with clearer content hierarchy and dedicated product discovery sections.
- Visual Presentation: Incorporated water-flow graphics, product-focused visuals and clearer product information sections.
- User Experience: Improved navigation, product education, customer support access and purchasing pathways.
- Mobile Experience: Added responsive navigation and a persistent two-button mobile call-to-action bar.
- Brand Consistency: Applied consistent colours, typography, spacing and visual elements throughout the redesigned homepage.
